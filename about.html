About Apna AI Studio

Apna AI Studio is a Hindi-friendly creative toolkit for YouTube creators and people exploring digital content creation.

What you can do

  • Draft starter ideas for Hindi video scripts.
  • Create photo prompts for image generators.
  • Create cinematic video prompts and choose video aspect ratios.
  • Record your own voice in a supported browser.
  • Explore third-party image-to-video services.

What the tools actually do

Some tools currently generate prompt templates in your browser rather than generating AI media themselves. The basic video maker creates a simple animated video from text and/or an uploaded image; it does not create authentic AI-generated scenes. External services have their own limits, availability and terms.

Our goal

Our goal is to make creative workflows easier to understand and use, especially for Hindi-speaking creators. We aim to explain limitations clearly and improve the tools over time.
